Light a row or column

Follow the rhythm

● Red● Orange● Yellow● Green● Cyan● Blue● Violet

Sound Waves shifts a rainbow wave pattern left and right to the music and changes rainbow colors only on detected beats. Sound Ring shows a rainbow outline with audio-driven spikes and cycling colors. Equalizer Bars shows regular rainbow bars rising from the bottom according to each bass-to-treble band's strength, with no fixed minimum height; silent bands turn off. The wave holds its position during silence, and the ring stays visible. Waves and ring add or remove up to 24 dots per update; equalizer bars follow their current audio heights directly. Flashing modes update their full pattern on detected beats, then turn off between beats. Flashing Bars uses rainbow colors. Ring and equalizer colors cycle each frame. Pixel updates never clear the whole curtain in one command. Complex frames may still take seconds to transmit; the preview animates faster than the curtain.

Connection & protocol settings

Close the Surplife app and any other apps connected to the curtain, then select it in the BLE picker. The controller automatically looks for compatible controls. Settings take effect on the next connection.

The original clock sample has two unexplained trailing bytes; the linked update uses a 10-byte timestamp. This controller uses the updated timestamp by default. Segmentation descriptions also differ; start with the 5-byte V0 header.

Color calibration

Test each physical channel and note its color, then select a mapping. “Save mapping” writes the selected order to the curtain’s memory.

Packet log

TX is a completed BLE write, not confirmation that the curtain applied it. DEMO packets never reach a device.
